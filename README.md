# BITEBACK

A course concept for student food feedback at a university mess. These are clickable prototypes, not a working product.

- **Smart Prompts** (main page): a mess entry scan triggers a one-tap lunch rating on the lock screen, with results shown on a mess team dashboard.
- **Original app** (`/original-app/`): the first version, designed as a standalone student app.

All ratings, dates and statistics are illustrative sample data. My Flame screens are a course concept, not official My Flame screens.
